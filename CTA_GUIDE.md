# PADO STORY CTA Guide

## Primary CTA

Use for direct purchase.

- Text: `바로 구매하기`
- Background: `var(--pado-teal)`
- Height: `52px` desktop, `50px` mobile
- Radius: `13px` to `16px`

## Secondary CTA

Use for cart.

- Text: `장바구니`
- Background: white
- Border: `var(--pado-line)`
- Text: `var(--pado-navy)`

## Footer CTA

Use after FAQ or long detail content.

- Include product name
- Include price
- Link back to purchase box
- Use navy-to-teal background
- Use coral button

## Disabled CTA

Sold out text:

- `재입고 안내 확인`

Never hide a disabled CTA without explaining why purchase is blocked.
