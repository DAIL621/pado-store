# PADO STORY Typography Guide

## Font Stack

`Pretendard, "Noto Sans KR", "Apple SD Gothic Neo", Arial, sans-serif`

## Product Detail Scale

| Element | Desktop | Mobile |
|---|---:|---:|
| Hero title | `36px` to `58px` | `31px` to `34px` |
| Section title | `26px` to `40px` | `24px` to `28px` |
| Body | `14px` to `16px` | `13px` to `15px` |
| Caption | `12px` to `13px` | `11px` to `12px` |
| Price | `34px` to `38px` | `29px` to `31px` |
| Button | `13px` to `15px` | `13px` to `14px` |

## Rules

- Korean headings use `word-break: keep-all`.
- Do not use negative letter spacing beyond `-0.055em`.
- Body line-height should be `1.6` to `1.85`.
- Price must be bold and visually stronger than badges.
- Section labels use uppercase English sparingly.
