# PADO STORY Mobile Guide

## Priority

Mobile is the primary buying environment.

## Breakpoints

- Small: `360px` to `389px`
- Standard: `390px` to `430px`
- Tablet: `768px` to `900px`

## Touch Targets

- Minimum height: `44px`
- Primary CTA: `50px` to `56px`
- Icon buttons: at least `42px`

## Detail Page Mobile Flow

1. Product image
2. Product name and price
3. Delivery promise
4. Option and CTA
5. Brand promise
6. Trust cards
7. Story and gallery

## Spacing

- Section vertical spacing: `34px` to `44px`
- Card gap: `8px` to `12px`
- Page side padding: `14px`

## Rules

- No horizontal overflow.
- Product image should not be hidden below brand copy.
- Sticky purchase action must not block important form controls.
- Long Korean words must use `word-break: keep-all` where appropriate.
