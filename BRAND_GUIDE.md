# PADO STORY Brand Guide

## Brand Essence

PADO STORY brings the freshness of the origin to the table.

Korean expression:

`산지의 오늘을 식탁까지.`

## Brand Keywords

- 산지
- 신선함
- 선별
- 냉장배송
- 실물촬영
- 믿을 수 있는 포장

## Brand Tone

- Confident but not loud
- Premium but practical
- Friendly but not cute
- Specific rather than abstract

## Required Brand Signals

Every product detail page should include:

- PADO STORY label
- Origin or producer context
- Delivery promise
- Fresh packaging promise
- Quality inspection signal

## Avoid

- Generic seafood market tone
- Excessive discount-only communication
- Overly decorative visuals
- Long brand story before product purchase information
